# Public selection ledger conventions

PUBLIC_FILE_SELECTION.csv records source identities as portable master-, rc01- or historical-relative identifiers, not personal absolute paths. Original and staged hashes distinguish unchanged copies, path/test adaptations and public PDF/TeX derivatives.

The ledger contains individual decisions for master inputs and current staged payloads. It is not a new copyright grant. Project copyright is unchanged; third-party notices retain their original scope. Data excluded from full scientific reproduction are not automatically required merely because a publication cites them.

MANIFEST.sha256 covers the complete distribution except itself. Administrative ledgers are not recursively hashed inside themselves; the complete manifest supplies those hashes.
