# Complete internal reproduction — not bundled

The public candidate cannot reproduce the complete agricultural coverage study from its included files. The following inputs are omitted intentionally:

| Needed for internal reproduction | Why absent |
|---|---|
| Seven-commune and agricultural mask geometry, source classifications and exact 1 km/500 m/250 m target weights | Provisional research database and upstream licensing/attribution review; not replaced by synthetic targets |
| WorldCover, WorldCereal, greenhouse archives/index, Sentinel bands/metadata, OSM landuse/boundaries and screening provenance | Large third-party data and asset-level redistribution obligations |
| Pinned leap seconds, planetary constants, DE405 and high-precision/predicted Earth-orientation kernels | Asset-specific redistribution/version review; no automatic downloads or substituted defaults |
| Full native nominal/refined/phase OEMs and state reports | Large internal reference products, separate provenance and archive exceptions |
| Full access events, exact/censored per-target gaps, rolling-coverage arrays and regression oracles | Not represented by the six-window saved point example |
| Original analytical reference bundle and 45-test suite | Depends on the above complete state/evidence assets and internal links |

The accepted private Master Release v1.0 supplies those inputs and its tested fresh-workspace workflow. This public candidate does not embed a machine-specific path to it or claim that arbitrary downloaded replacements reproduce its hashes.

The internal order is: native GUI mission → finalized explicitly selected OEM → frame/time/kernel validation → same-epoch native-state comparison → three agricultural targets → whole ROI and statistics → separate analytical verification. A changed OEM/configuration/code/geographic fingerprint invalidates the relevant regression gate; stale access caches are not accepted.

Documented internal reproduction demonstrates numerical consistency, not independent physical truth. The public 39-test suite is a different, smaller scope. The broader phase/swath/EOP/alternative-mask sensitivities remain archived findings, not studies performed for this candidate.
