# Excluded data — no publication-document hold

Documents 2A RevD3.4, 2A-1 RevD3.5 and 2A-2 v0.2.1 are included as reviewed public-distribution derivatives with paired TeX and shared figure dependencies.

The complete agricultural target database, raw/derived source rasters/vectors, kernels, full native states/access histories and internal review/log archives remain outside this public subset. They are not necessary to rebuild the publication PDFs. Their absence limits full scientific reproduction, not publication of rendered figures carrying the applicable credits.

No new project licence is requested or imposed. Public repository publication still awaits the author's separate manual approval.
